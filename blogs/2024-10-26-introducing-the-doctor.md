The reason why all this exists As a massive fan of Doctor Who , a ‘Whovian’ as we’re called, I had already picked the name for this product years ago. I can’t think of a better name for what the Doctor does. In the Doctor Who universe, The Doctor is a time lord that travels the universe throughout all time and space, helping people in need and standing up for the little guy.
